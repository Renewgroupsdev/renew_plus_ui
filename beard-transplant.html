OVERVIEW

What is Beard Transplant?

Beard transplant is an advanced restoration technique that uses your own hair follicles to create a natural-looking, fuller beard that blends with your facial features.

THE PROCEDURE

How Beard Transplant works

  1. 01

    Using FUE technology, individual follicles are extracted from a donor area and precisely implanted into the beard region.

  2. 02

    The process is minimally invasive and leaves no visible scarring.

WHO IS IT FOR

Suitable candidates

Ideal for men with patchy, thin or sparse beards who want permanent density.

Results vary from person to person. Your specialist will confirm the right plan after examining you.

WHY CHOOSE IT

Key benefits

  • Beard design tailored to your face shape and style
  • Transplanted hair blends with existing facial hair
  • Permanent – grafted follicles keep growing for life
  • Most patients resume normal activities within days
  • Boosts facial aesthetics and self-assurance
  • Scar-free with the advanced FUE technique

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How many grafts are needed?

Between 1,000 and 4,000+, decided after an analysis of your hair loss and the density you want.

Is the procedure painful?

FUE is performed under local anaesthesia and is mostly painless, with minimal discomfort.

What are the side effects?

Temporary redness, swelling or mild itching, which resolve within days with proper aftercare.

When do results appear?

Growth starts at 3–4 months, with visible improvement by 6 months and complete results by 8–12 months.

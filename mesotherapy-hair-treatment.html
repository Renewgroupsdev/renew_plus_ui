OVERVIEW

What is Mesotherapy Hair?

Advanced Mesotherapy is a minimally invasive procedure that addresses hair loss and thinning by delivering essential nutrients directly to the scalp, strengthening follicles and promoting regrowth.

THE PROCEDURE

How it works

Trained specialists inject a customised blend of vitamins, minerals and growth-promoting substances into the scalp. A numbing solution can be used for comfort. Sessions are brief and need no downtime.

WHY CHOOSE IT

Key benefits

  • Activates dormant hair follicles for natural regrowth
  • Reduces hair fall caused by stress, hormones and poor nutrition
  • Increases hair thickness and density over time
  • Creates a healthy scalp environment for growth
  • Improves the overall look of hair and scalp

WHO IS IT FOR

Suitable candidates

Best for early-stage thinning, stress-related hair loss or hormonal hair fall – and when the follicles are still active.

Results vary from person to person. Your specialist will confirm the right plan after examining you.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Is mesotherapy painful?

Discomfort is minimal thanks to the numbing solution applied beforehand.

What are the side effects?

Mild redness, swelling or slight discomfort may occur temporarily and usually settle quickly.

Can it regrow hair in completely bald areas?

It stimulates growth only where follicles are still active; completely bald areas may not respond.

How does it compare with PRP?

Mesotherapy delivers external nutrients, while PRP uses the platelets from your own blood for regeneration.

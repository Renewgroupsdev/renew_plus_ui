OUR SUCCESS STORY

Safe, effective, personalised care

Renew Plus Hair and Skin Care provides personalised hair and skin treatments for men and women across Tamil Nadu and Bengaluru, with a focus on safe, effective results that enhance confidence.

From hair transplant, PRP and GFC therapy to laser care, skin rejuvenation and acne & scar treatment, every plan is built around your scalp, your skin and your goals — and every journey starts with an honest consultation.

Today Renew Plus is a growing network of clinics, making advanced hair restoration and skin care accessible to more people in more cities.

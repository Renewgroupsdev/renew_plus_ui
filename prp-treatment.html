OVERVIEW

What is PRP?

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) hair treatment is a natural, non-surgical solution that uses your body’s own platelets to stimulate hair growth. It is a minimally invasive procedure that targets hair loss, thinning and scalp health concerns.

THE PROCEDURE

How it works

Platelets are separated from a small sample of your blood and injected into the scalp to activate the hair follicles. A numbing cream is applied beforehand to minimise discomfort during the injections.

WHY CHOOSE IT

Key benefits

  • Natural regrowth using your body’s own growth factors
  • Strengthens hair, reduces hair fall and improves thickness
  • No cuts or stitches required
  • Resume daily activities straight away
  • Quick, comfort-focused procedure
  • Works for men and women with thinning or receding hairlines

WHO IS IT FOR

Suitable candidates

PRP works best for people with active hair follicles who are experiencing early hair loss or thinning. Completely bald areas may see limited results.

Results vary from person to person. Your specialist will confirm the right plan after examining you.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

How much does PRP cost?

The cost varies with the number of sessions and your individual condition. We share a clear estimate after your consultation.

Are there side effects?

Temporary redness or swelling may occur and usually subsides quickly.

Is PRP effective for everyone?

It is most beneficial in the early stages of hair loss, while follicles are still active.

PRP or GFC – which is better?

Both are effective. GFC offers a more refined concentration of growth factors; your specialist will recommend the right option for you.

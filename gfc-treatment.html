OVERVIEW

What is GFC?

GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate) is a non-surgical hair restoration treatment that uses concentrated growth factors derived from your own blood to stimulate hair follicles, reduce hair loss and improve hair density over time.

Concentrated growth factors from your own blood to wake up weak hair follicles.

THE PROCEDURE

How GFC works

  1. 01

    A small blood sample is taken and processed into a concentrated serum of growth factors.

  2. 02

    This refined preparation is then delivered into the areas of thinning to support follicle activity and promote regrowth.

  3. 03

    A topical numbing cream keeps the session comfortable.

WHO IS IT FOR

Suitable candidates

Best for people with weakened or miniaturised follicles that are still active. Suitability depends on the cause and stage of hair loss and is confirmed at your consultation.

Results vary from person to person. Your specialist will confirm the right plan after examining you.

WHY CHOOSE IT

Key benefits

  • Faster regrowth – highly concentrated growth factors stimulate follicles quickly
  • Reduces ongoing hair fall from the early sessions
  • Improves hair thickness and density
  • Uses only naturally derived components – no chemicals
  • Minimal recovery – return to normal activities immediately
  • More refined than traditional PRP, with a reduced inflammatory response

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Is GFC effective for hair fall?

Yes. GFC delivers a concentrated dose of growth factors that helps reduce hair fall and supports regrowth in active follicles.

How is GFC different from PRP?

Both use your own blood. GFC is a more refined, concentrated preparation of growth factors, with a reduced inflammatory response.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on your hair loss pattern and scalp condition. Our specialist builds a personalised plan at the consultation.

Are there side effects?

GFC is generally well tolerated. Temporary redness, mild swelling or tenderness can occur and usually settles quickly.

OVERVIEW

What is Hydrafacial?

Hydrafacial is a non-invasive treatment that deeply cleanses, exfoliates and intensely hydrates your skin, with immediate results and no downtime.

THE PROCEDURE

How it works

The treatment combines deep cleansing, gentle exfoliation and impurity extraction, then infuses nourishing serums into the skin. Each session is personalised to your skin type and concerns.

WHY CHOOSE IT

Key benefits

  • Instant radiance and glow
  • Deep pore cleansing and removal of dead skin
  • Intense hydration and moisture restoration
  • Helps reduce acne and pigmentation
  • Painless with zero downtime
  • Customisable for every skin type

WHO IS IT FOR

Suitable candidates

Oily, dry, sensitive and combination skin. Ideal for dullness, dehydration, clogged pores, uneven texture and acne.

Results vary from person to person. Your specialist will confirm the right plan after examining you.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Is it painful?

No. It is a gentle, comfortable procedure with no pain or discomfort.

Does it help acne and pigmentation?

Yes. It clears pores, reduces acne and improves skin tone for a more even complexion.

How long do results last?

Around 5–7 days, with better benefits from consistent sessions.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. Treatments are customised for all skin types, including sensitive skin.
